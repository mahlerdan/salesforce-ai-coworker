# Video 1: "I Built Myself an AI Coworker for Salesforce"

**Format:** Hook / demo reel. 4-6 minutes.
**Goal:** Make people say "I want that." No setup, no tutorial — just show it working.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

> "I'm a Salesforce consultant. I spend my days in orgs, Slack, meetings, and documentation —
> same as most of you. A few months ago I started building something that's changed how I work.
>
> I'm not a YouTuber. I'm not selling a course. I just built something useful and I want to
> share it.
>
> I built myself an AI coworker. His name is Scout."

*[Cut to: Slack interface, Scout responding to a message]*

---

## Demo 1: Meeting Notes → Requirements (90 seconds)

> "Here's a real example. I just got out of a client meeting. I have rough notes —
> half-sentences, bullet points, the usual mess."

*[Show: pasting meeting notes into Slack DM with the agent]*

> "I tell Scout to turn these into requirements."

*[Show: the agent processing, then outputting structured user stories with acceptance criteria,
implementation notes, and open questions]*

> "User stories, acceptance criteria, Salesforce implementation notes, and a list of things
> I need to follow up on. That used to take me 30-45 minutes. Now it takes about 2."

---

## Demo 2: Slack Monitoring (60 seconds)

> "I also have an agent that monitors my Slack. Meetings, client channels, DMs — she reads
> everything I've approved her to read and pulls out what I actually need to know."

*[Show: a digest output — action items, decisions made, things needing follow-up]*

> "I come in Monday morning and instead of scrolling through 200 messages trying to figure
> out what I missed, I get this. Action items with owners, decisions that were made, and
> things that need my attention — prioritized."

---

## Demo 3: Salesforce Troubleshooting (60 seconds)

> "Last week I hit a governor limit issue in a client's org. Instead of spending 20 minutes
> digging through debug logs and Stack Exchange, I pasted the error into Scout."

*[Show: pasting an error, agent walking through diagnosis]*

> "Root cause, explanation, fix, and a note about what to watch for next time. I still
> review everything — Scout doesn't push to production. But the research and diagnosis
> that used to take half an hour happens in a minute."

---

## The Bigger Picture (60 seconds)

> "What makes this different from ChatGPT or Agentforce or Copilot? Context.
>
> Scout knows my projects. Knows the orgs I work in. Knows my role. Has skills built
> specifically for Salesforce work — not generic AI, but an agent that understands
> the actual job.
>
> And it runs in Slack, where I already work. I'm not switching to another app.
> I'm talking to a coworker."

---

## Close (30 seconds)

> "I'm going to show you how to build your own. The next video walks through the full
> setup — from zero to a working AI coworker in Slack.
>
> I've also open-sourced the starter kit — SOUL template, Salesforce skills, setup
> guide, everything. Link in the description.
>
> I'm not a content creator. I'm a Salesforce professional who built something that
> makes my job easier, and I think it could help you too."

*[End card: Subscribe, link to repo, "Next: Building Scout from scratch"]*

---

## Production Notes

- **Screen recordings needed:** 3 Slack demos (meeting notes, digest, troubleshooting)
- **Prep:** Have real (or realistic sanitized) meeting notes, a digest output, and a
  troubleshooting exchange ready. Don't fake it — use actual workflows with client
  names/details scrubbed.
- **Tone:** Conversational, not polished. You're a consultant sharing your setup, not
  a tech influencer. Authenticity > production value.
- **Music:** Light background if any. Don't overdo it.
- **Thumbnail:** Split screen — you on one side, Slack with Scout on the other.
  Text: "I Built an AI Coworker for Salesforce"
