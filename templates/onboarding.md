# Onboarding: Configure Your AI Coworker

> Use these questions to configure your agent's SOUL and initial skills. Your agent can walk you through these interactively, or you can fill them out and paste the answers.

---

## About You

1. **What's your name?**

2. **What's your primary Salesforce role?**
   - [ ] Admin
   - [ ] Business Analyst
   - [ ] Developer
   - [ ] Architect
   - [ ] Consultant
   - [ ] Team Lead / Manager
   - [ ] Other: ___

3. **What Salesforce products do you work with regularly?**
   (Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, CPQ, Marketing Cloud, Platform, Industries, etc.)

4. **What's your technical depth?**
   - [ ] Declarative only (Flows, config, reports)
   - [ ] Declarative + some code (read Apex, write simple triggers)
   - [ ] Full-stack (Apex, LWC, integrations, CI/CD)
   - [ ] Architecture level (design systems, not just build them)

---

## Your Work

5. **What does a typical work week look like?**
   (Meetings, development, requirements gathering, troubleshooting, documentation, client calls, etc.)

6. **What are your 3 biggest time sinks?**
   (The things that eat hours but feel like they shouldn't)

7. **What repetitive tasks do you do weekly?**
   (Status updates, meeting summaries, Jira/story creation, deployment checklists, etc.)

8. **What tools do you use daily besides Salesforce?**
   (Slack, Jira, Confluence, VS Code, GitHub, Google Docs, Notion, email, etc.)

9. **Where do your meeting notes / transcripts live?**
   (tl;dv, Otter, Fireflies, Google Docs, Notion, manual notes, etc.)

---

## Your Projects

10. **How many active projects are you working on right now?**

11. **For each project, briefly describe:**
    - Project name
    - Your role on it
    - Salesforce org (prod, sandbox, dev)
    - Key stakeholders
    - Current phase (discovery, build, testing, support)

---

## Agent Boundaries

12. **What should your agent do without asking?**
    (Research, organize notes, draft documentation, troubleshoot errors, etc.)

13. **What should your agent ALWAYS ask before doing?**
    (Anything in production, contacting people, making commitments, etc.)

14. **What systems can your agent access?**
    (Slack read-only, Jira read/write, Salesforce sandbox only, etc.)

15. **What must your agent NEVER access or do?**
    (Production DML, client emails, financial systems, etc.)

---

## Quick Wins

16. **If your agent could do ONE thing really well starting tomorrow, what would it be?**

17. **What's something you had to explain to a coworker recently that you wish was already documented?**

18. **What meeting do you have this week that would benefit from an AI reviewing the notes afterward?**

---

## Output

Based on your answers, your agent (or you) can generate:

- [ ] A customized SOUL.md
- [ ] Initial project context files
- [ ] Recommended skills to enable first
- [ ] Tool/integration priorities
- [ ] A week-1 plan for getting value from your agent
